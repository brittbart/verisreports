About Verum Signal

Independent · Self-funded · Colorado

What we do

Verum Signal breaks news articles and live political debates into the individual, checkable claims they make. It checks each claim against independent sources and publishes the result claim by claim, with the sources behind it. Across many articles, those results add up to a reliability profile for each outlet.

Claims made. Evidence checked. You decide.

How it works

Every claim goes through the same process, under a published, versioned methodology. AI models extract and check claims following the methodology's rules, and each verdict is published with its reasoning and sources. The process doesn't take an outlet's political leaning into account and applies the same standard to every outlet. How it works walks through each step.

Who's behind it

Verum Signal is an independent publisher based in Colorado. It was founded in 2026.

Independence and funding

Verum Signal is self-funded. It carries no advertising and takes no money from any outlet or campaign it rates. It has no political affiliation and does not rate candidates or endorse parties.

Corrections and disputes

If you believe a verdict, a speaker attribution or a claim's wording is wrong, tell us through the dispute form or at disputes@verumsignal.com. The corrections policy explains what happens next.
