Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026 · Verum Signal

Verum Signal is built to collect as little about you as it can. It doesn't sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Visiting the website

When you view a page, we record the page, the page that linked you there, your browser type, screen width, time on page, and a random ID that lasts only while your browser tab is open. Your network (IP) address is stored only in a one-way scrambled form. We keep these records for 90 days. After that, only daily totals remain: how many views each page had and which sites sent visitors, with nothing that identifies you.

To protect the site from abuse, your network address is held for about 10 minutes.

Accounts and paid plans

If you create an account, we keep your email address. Sign-in links expire, and a cookie keeps you signed in. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number; we keep only the customer and subscription references Stripe gives us.

The browser extension

The extension sends only the website's name (for example, example.com) to look up that outlet's profile. These lookups aren't stored. If you click "Analyze this article", the extension sends that article's address so we can analyze it.

Article reports

When you ask for a report on an article, we fetch and analyze it. Reports are public and never show who requested them. If you're signed in, the request counts toward your plan's usage.

Developer API

API keys are linked to the email address that requested them. Each API call is logged (endpoint, result, time, and your network address in scrambled form) for 90 days. Monthly call counts are kept for billing.

The mobile app

If you turn on notifications, we store a device identifier, a notification token and your device model, and use them only to send the notifications you chose.

Disputes, the waitlist and API access requests

We keep your email address and what you send us. Dispute submissions and contact details are never published, and a dispute is deleted two years after it has been reviewed.

Service providers

We use Railway (hosting and database), Stripe (payments), Expo (app notifications), Anthropic (AI models that analyze article text), a web-content retrieval service (fetching articles) and an email delivery service (sign-in links). Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your network address when your browser fetches them.

Cookies

One cookie, only if you sign in. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Your choices

Email privacy@verumsignal.com to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to close your account.

Children

Verum Signal isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, we'll update the date above and post a notice on the home page.
